Why This Matters

Lenders have
a checklist.

Before any bank or lender approves you, they verify your business is legitimate. Most entrepreneurs lose funding opportunities because they aren't set up right.

01

Your LLC must be active

Lenders won't fund a sole proprietorship. Your LLC needs to be properly formed with a registered agent on file.

02

Your business must be findable

Lenders Google your business. No website, no phone listing, no address — no funding.

03

Separation is required

Your business must be completely separate — separate address, phone, email, and banking.

04

Structure = fundability

A business set up correctly from day one qualifies for significantly more funding than one patched together over time.
