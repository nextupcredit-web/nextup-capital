Why This Matters

Lenders have
a checklist.

Before any bank or lender approves you for business funding, they verify your business is legitimate. Most entrepreneurs lose funding opportunities because they aren't set up right. We fix that.

01

Your LLC must be active

Lenders won't fund a sole proprietorship. Your LLC needs to be properly formed with a registered agent on file.

02

Your business must be findable

Lenders Google your business. No website, no phone listing, no address — no funding. Presence is everything.

03

Separation is required

Your business must be completely separate from you personally — separate address, phone, email, and banking.

04

Structure = fundability

A business that's set up correctly from day one qualifies for significantly more funding than one that's been patched together over time.
