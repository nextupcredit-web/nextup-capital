Why This Matters

Lenders have
a checklist.

Before any bank or lender approves you, they run a verification process. Most entrepreneurs get denied not because of credit — but because their business wasn't set up right.

01

Your LLC must be active and compliant

Lenders won't fund sole proprietorships. Your LLC needs a registered agent on file and proper documentation.

02

Your business must be findable

Lenders Google your business. No website, no phone listing, no address — no funding. Presence is everything.

03

Everything must be separate

Your business needs its own address, phone, email, and banking — completely separate from your personal life.

04

Structure determines fundability

A business structured correctly from day one qualifies for significantly more than one pieced together over time.
